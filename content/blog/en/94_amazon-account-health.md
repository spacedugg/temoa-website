# Amazon Account Health: prioritise risks before sales are interrupted

Amazon Account Health belongs in ongoing operational management. A notice may concern one document, one ASIN or a wider account issue. Teams lose valuable time when they begin finding owners and evidence only after a restriction appears.

A favourable overall rating should not replace inspection of specific notices. The Account Health Rating covers defined policy risks; it is not a comprehensive guarantee that every product, document and process is compliant.

## Turn notices into actionable records

Capture the complete message, marketplace, affected offers, current status, platform deadline and responsible person. Link to the original case and list the exact documents requested. Keep Amazon's deadline separate from your own internal response target.

| Issue | First question | Operational owner |
|---|---|---|
| Missing product evidence | Which document and variation are specified? | Product and compliance teams |
| Conflicting catalogue data | Which approved source is correct? | Catalogue and product teams |
| Rights complaint | What claim and offer are involved? | Account team with legal review |
| Delivery problem | Is the cause stock, process or an isolated order? | Logistics and account team |

## Prioritise exposure without ignoring urgency

Safety, binding deadlines and the severity of a notice may override commercial size. Use financial exposure to plan capacity, not to justify leaving smaller issues unresolved.

Add dependencies such as shared documentation across variants, planned promotions and inbound stock. An apparently narrow case can require coordination across several teams.

## Make evidence retrievable before it is needed

Organise relevant documents by product, version, supplier and variation. Confirm that the evidence matches the goods actually sold. A folder alone cannot determine what is legally or operationally required, but it can reduce avoidable search time.

Name one case owner. That person should distinguish confirmed facts from open questions and coordinate the response requested by Amazon. Do not submit conflicting explanations from several teams or assume every notice requires the same lengthy action plan.

## Close the process gap after resolution

Ask whether the problem began at product setup, data synchronisation or an unowned handover. Convert the answer into a specific process change. Useful internal measures include unresolved urgent cases, first-review time, completed deadlines and repeated causes; these are separate from Amazon's own account metrics.

Connect a weekly risk review to launches, catalogue changes and inventory plans.
