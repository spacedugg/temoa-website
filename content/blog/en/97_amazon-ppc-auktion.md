# Amazon PPC auction: what a bid really costs

A base bid is a control, not a complete explanation of the final CPC. Sponsored Products offers fixed and dynamic bidding, as well as placement and other applicable adjustments. The useful question is how much a particular ASIN can afford to pay for a click from a particular search intent.

## Calculate the economic ceiling first

Deduct product cost, referral and fulfilment fees, and expected variable returns cost from attributable order revenue. If €12 remains before advertising on a €40 order, a ten percent click-to-order conversion implies a simplified €1.20 break-even cost per click. Customer acquisition may justify another threshold, but make that decision explicit.

<aside class="article-example"><p class="example-label">Illustrative economics</p><h3>A more expensive click can yield a cheaper order</h3><p>Placement A costs €1 per click and converts at 7%, giving €14.29 ad cost per order. Placement B costs €1.40 and converts at 12%, giving €11.67 per order. With €12 pre-ad contribution, B is near break-even while A is not. Validate sample size, attribution and incrementality before changing the portfolio.</p></aside>

## Audit in sequence

Separate branded, generic, competitor and product targets. Export search terms, placements, bids, CPC, clicks and attributed sales for the same period. Identify whether a change came from CPC, conversion or order value. Test one material lever at a time, and inspect stock, price and Featured Offer status. Avoid turning off discovery from a very short zero-sales window.
