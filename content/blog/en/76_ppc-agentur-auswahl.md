# Choosing an Amazon PPC agency: what matters in an advertising specialist

Amazon advertising is complex and largely decides visibility and profitability. No surprise, then, that many brands consider a specialist PPC agency. But the label says nothing about quality on its own. This neutral guide shows what really matters when choosing an advertising specialist, with no ranking and with clear criteria.

## When a PPC agency makes sense

A specialist PPC agency pays off above all when your ad budget has reached a size at which professional management makes a noticeable difference, when you lack the time or the specialist knowledge for clean campaign work, or when your ACoS is running away from you and you cannot find the cause. On a very small budget the fees often exceed the value that can be added.

## Specialist or full service?

A basic decision is whether you want a pure PPC specialist or a full-service provider. A specialist brings deep advertising expertise but works in isolation from listing, creative and account management, even though those areas are tightly bound to advertising performance (a weak listing ruins even the best campaign). A full-service provider thinks in a more integrated way and is not always as deep in PPC. Which fits depends on how well your other areas are already set up.

## The most important metrics and criteria

With an advertising specialist, watch for the following in particular:

- **Understanding of ACoS *and* TACoS:** a good team does not only optimise ACoS, it keeps total revenue and the organic effect (TACoS) in view.
- **Campaign structure competence:** ask how they structure accounts. Clean architecture is the basis for scaling.
- **A data-led way of working:** regular search term analysis, negative keyword maintenance, a clear optimisation logic.
- **Transparent reporting:** you have to understand what is being done, why, and how the metrics develop.
- **Margin awareness:** a good team knows your margin and optimises for profit rather than for revenue or a pretty ACoS.

## The right questions

Ask concrete questions. How will you structure my campaigns? How do you handle rising click prices? Do you optimise for ACoS or for profitability? How often do you adjust bids and negative keywords? How do you measure the influence of advertising on organic revenue? The answers quickly show whether someone thinks strategically or only turns dials.

## Warning signs

Be sceptical of guaranteed ACoS or revenue figures (impossible to promise seriously in an auction environment), of a pure focus on a low ACoS with no margin or TACoS context, of opaque methods and of missing concrete case examples. If a provider ignores the organic effect of advertising, a central piece of the strategy is missing.

## Conclusion

Choosing a PPC agency means bringing an advertising specialist onto a decisive lever of your business. Look for strategic thinking (ACoS *and* TACoS, margin awareness, clean campaign structure), transparency and demonstrable results, and settle whether a specialist or an integrated full-service solution better fits where you are. Recognise guarantee promises and pure ACoS fixation as warning signs, ask the right questions, and you find a partner who runs advertising as a profitable growth driver rather than a cost centre.
