# Amazon DSP: budgeting for a test that can support a decision

Amazon DSP becomes relevant when your brand has a specific audience or reach question that its existing advertising cannot answer. The entry price matters less than whether the available budget can produce useful observations. Define the business decision before comparing proposals.

## Separate access from total investment

Amazon describes a typical minimum of US$50,000 for its managed service, with variation by country. This is not a universal minimum for every partner-operated arrangement. Ask which access model is proposed and what the fee includes.

| Cost | Clarification to request |
|---|---|
| Media | Actual spend available for delivery |
| Management | Fixed fee, percentage or combination |
| Creative | Formats, variants and revisions included |
| Measurement | Available analysis and its limitations |

The same total budget can buy very different amounts of media. Keep click-attributed and view-attributed results understandable, and distinguish platform reporting from a full contribution calculation.

## Start with one test question

Choose an audience, a product group and a decision criterion. Document the creative, duration, spend limit and changes that would invalidate the comparison. Combining acquisition, retargeting and awareness in a small test makes a blended ROAS difficult to interpret.

<aside class="article-example"><p class="example-label">Planning example · not a benchmark</p><h3>Four audiences can leave too little evidence per group</h3><p>€6,000 of media at an assumed €12 CPM gives 500,000 impressions. At 0.2% click-through and 5% orders per click, the model produces 1,000 clicks and 50 orders. Four equal cells would average just 12.5 orders each. Adding €1,500 management and €1,000 creative brings total investment to €8,500. None of these inputs is a forecast.</p></aside>

Repeat the calculation with less favourable assumptions. If even the favourable scenario cannot support the intended commercial decision, change the test design before spending.

## Match measurement to the objective

Reach and frequency can describe delivery. They cannot establish additional sales. New-to-Brand also does not prove that advertising caused a purchase. An [incrementality assessment](/blog/amazon-werbung-inkrementalitaet) requires an appropriate comparison and explicit assumptions.

Confirm the availability of audience and measurement features for your access model. Do not promise an experiment that the account cannot actually run. Separate exploratory learning from evidence strong enough to justify sustained investment.

## Set readiness and stop criteria

Weak availability, unclear contribution or unresolved listing problems can undermine interpretation. Assign an owner for price, content and stock changes during the test. Stop conditions can include delivery errors, unsuitable traffic, threatened availability or an exhausted test limit.
