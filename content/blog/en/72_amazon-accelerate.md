# Amazon Accelerate: what the seller conference delivers and how to use it

> **Market note:** Amazon Accelerate is primarily a **US seller conference**. For the German and European market, local formats and programmes matter as well (Amazon events, webinars and the Amazon Academy in the EU). Many of the features shown there reach Europe on a delay.

Once a year Amazon gathers its seller community for a major conference: Amazon Accelerate. For many brands it is the most important event of the year, because new tools, programmes and strategic directions are presented there, often things that shape selling in the year ahead. This article explains what Accelerate is and how to use it without necessarily attending in person.

## What Amazon Accelerate is

Amazon Accelerate is an annual seller-focused conference at which Amazon presents what is new in its seller ecosystem. At the centre are announcements about new tools, programmes, advertising formats, logistics and AI features, along with insight into Amazon's strategic direction. Talks, workshops and networking come with it. The exact dates and formats (in person and digital) vary from year to year, so check the current information in good time.

## Why it matters for sellers

The real value of Accelerate sits in the announcements. Amazon uses the stage to present features that are often rolled out shortly afterwards. Knowing early what is coming, new advertising options, AI-supported tools, logistics programmes or changes to fees and policies, lets you prepare rather than be surprised. In a market where adapting early is an advantage, that knowledge is worth money.

## Typical themes

Even though the content differs each year, Accelerate announcements often circle recurring fields: advertising and new ad formats, AI features for listing creation and buyer interaction, logistics and FBA programmes, tools for brand building and brand protection, analytics and data, and seller support programmes. Know those fields and you can classify announcements quickly and check them for relevance to your own business.

## How to benefit without attending

You do not have to be there in person to benefit. Follow the announcements through official Amazon channels, industry media and post-event summaries. After Accelerate, make a list of the changes relevant to your category, your model and your size, and check deliberately which ones you want to test early. Early adopters of new tools often have a head start before a format gets crowded.

## Judging announcements properly

Not every change is relevant to you, and not everything presented with a shine delivers in daily use what it promises. Judge each announcement soberly: does it solve a real problem in my business? Does it fit my size and my model? Is adopting it early worth the effort? That keeps you from chasing every new tool and lets you concentrate on what genuinely helps.

## Conclusion

Amazon Accelerate is the annual shop window for the future of selling on Amazon, with announcements on tools, advertising, logistics and AI that shape the coming year. The greatest benefit lies in knowing early what is coming and preparing deliberately. You do not have to be there: follow the announcements, check them for relevance to your business and test the promising ones early, and you gain a head start. Since dates and content change every year, keep an eye on the current information in good time.
