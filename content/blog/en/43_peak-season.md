# Amazon peak season: how to prepare for Q4

For many Amazon sellers the entire year is decided in a few weeks: around Prime Day, Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Christmas. Demand explodes in peak season, and only those who prepared in time benefit. Start planning in November and you are already late. This article covers what preparation needs to include.

## Why peak season decides everything

In the high season more people buy, with a higher willingness to buy, in a shorter window. That means enormous opportunity, and also sharper competition, higher click prices and no mercy for mistakes. Selling out at the wrong moment costs you not just the peak revenue but the ranking you then have to win back the hard way. Peak season amplifies good preparation and neglect alike.

## Stock: the most important lever

The most common and most expensive mistake is running out during the high season. Plan your stock early and generously:

- **Estimate demand realistically:** work from last year's data and your current growth rate. Expect considerably higher unit sales than in normal trading.
- **Allow for lead times:** production and shipping (especially from overseas) take longer in the high season, and supply chains run at capacity.
- **Watch the inbound cut-offs:** Amazon sets deadlines by which FBA goods must have arrived to be available on time. Those deadlines are hard, so build in a buffer.
- **Check your storage limits:** make sure your IPI score and storage capacity are sufficient to hold enough goods.

## Optimise listings in good time

Do not change listings in the middle of the hot phase. That risks ranking swings at the worst possible moment. Get images, title, bullet points and A+ content into shape beforehand so your increased traffic converts as well as it can. Every improvement in conversion works especially hard in peak season, simply because so many more visitors arrive.

## Prepare and scale up advertising

Click prices rise noticeably in the high season. Get your campaigns ready in time: raise budgets, identify the profitable keywords, maintain negative keywords and set daily budgets so you do not run dry on the biggest days. If you have well-ranking products, build visibility early in the season rather than starting on Black Friday when everyone is bidding.

## Plan deals and promotions

Formats such as Lightning Deals, coupons and discount promotions can give strong pushes in peak season, and they often have to be submitted and qualified weeks in advance. Plan which products you want to promote when, and price the promotions so the margin still holds after the discount and the higher ad costs.

## Be operationally ready

More sales mean more customer enquiries, more returns and more potential problems. Make sure customer service can respond quickly, have your returns processes ready and keep an eye on your metrics. A drop in performance does particular damage in the high season.

## Conclusion

Peak season is the most important stretch of the Amazon year, and it is won in summer and early autumn rather than in December. What decides it is sufficient stock, planned around lead times and inbound deadlines. Add listings optimised in good time, advertising prepared with higher budgets, planned deals and an operation that can take the load. Plan early and thoroughly and the high season becomes the strongest part of your year instead of a series of expensive misses.
