# Business structure for Amazon: trade, accounts and VAT

A commercial Amazon business needs an appropriate legal and tax structure. That does not automatically require a GmbH or US LLC. Conversely, Amazon's Individual selling plan does not make a commercial trader a private consumer seller.

## Separate four decisions

Define the actual activity, choose an appropriate legal form, establish required registrations and map where goods will be stored and sold. The occasional sale of personal used goods differs from systematic trade. A fixed Amazon unit count is not the sole legal test.

A company with limited liability does not remove every possible personal exposure. The choice depends on the business, funding and responsibilities, and should be assessed with qualified advisers.

## OSS is not a universal replacement for local registration

The EU One-Stop-Shop simplifies reporting for specified cross-border B2C transactions. Own stock and movements into another country can create additional local obligations. Assess the intended storage model before enabling international fulfilment.

> **Practical preparation:** Draw the warehouse countries, stock movements, selling channels and legal entities on one page. Give that map to the tax adviser before activating a new fulfilment programme.

## Keep account evidence consistent

Maintain the legal name, address, registration and tax details, beneficial owners and bank information consistently across documents and the account. Use user permissions for agency access. A legal-form change needs a planned account update; changing a brand label is not enough.
