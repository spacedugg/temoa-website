# Amazon gift returns: how they work and what sellers should know

Gifts often get exchanged, and Amazon has a dedicated process for it: the gift return. It works differently from a normal return, above all because the recipient is not the buyer. For sellers it is useful to understand how it runs and what it means for them. This article gives the overview.

## What a gift return is

If an item is bought marked as a gift and sent with a gift option, the **recipient** can return it without the original buyer, the person giving the gift, finding out or getting the purchase price refunded. Instead the recipient usually receives a refund as Amazon credit (a gift card) rather than a repayment to the buyer's payment method. That keeps the gift discreet, and the giver notices nothing of the exchange.

## Why it exists

The purpose is discretion and convenience. Nobody wants to embarrass the person who gave the gift, and the recipient should be able to turn something unsuitable into something they want without fuss. For Amazon it is a service that makes gifting through the platform more attractive, which matters particularly in the gift season around the holidays.

## What it means for sellers

From a seller's perspective a gift return is essentially a return like any other, with the same questions about the condition of the goods and whether they can be resold. Under **FBA**, Amazon handles the process largely on its own, the refund to the recipient included. Under **FBM** you are more closely involved. What matters is knowing that the refund in a gift return typically goes to the recipient as credit, while the commercial treatment on your side follows the usual returns rules.

## Plan for the seasonal spike

Gift returns naturally cluster after the Christmas season. Amazon often extends the return windows for holiday purchases during that period, so returns, gift returns included, can arrive weeks after the holidays. Build that into your inventory and cash planning: part of your strong Q4 sales comes back as returns in the new year.

## How to handle them well

In practice, gift returns follow the same principles as returns generally: run a smooth process, check the condition of goods coming back and handle resellable items correctly. Handling returns well, even when the recipient is not a regular customer, pays into your reviews and your reputation, because the recipient can become a buyer too. For the restocking fee the usual rules apply: only where there is a genuine loss in value, and never as a punishment.

## Conclusion

The gift return is Amazon's discreet exchange route for recipients: the recipient can return a gift, usually receives Amazon credit, and the giver stays out of it. For sellers it is essentially a return with the usual questions about condition and resale, handled by Amazon under FBA and by you under FBM. Plan for the seasonal spike after the holidays, handle returns professionally in general, and gift returns present no difficulty.
