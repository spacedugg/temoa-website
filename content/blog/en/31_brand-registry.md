# Amazon Brand Registry: enrolment, benefits and how to protect your brand

For a brand selling in Germany and other European Amazon stores, Brand Registry connects trademark evidence, catalogue roles and tools for reporting substantiated infringement. Enrolment is one operational step; it does not itself settle every listing conflict or grant exclusive control over legitimate resale. This guide separates eligibility, day-to-day catalogue work and actual protection cases.

## What Brand Registry is

Amazon Brand Registry is a programme for brand owners that gives you more control over your product listings and provides tools to protect your brand. Eligibility depends on the trademark and role. Check the rights and available functions in the relevant brand-owner account for each marketplace.

## What you need

The central requirement is a **registered trademark** (or in certain cases a pending application, depending on the current programme rules). Usually you need:

- an active, registered word or figurative mark with the relevant trademark office,
- the registration number,
- products or packaging that visibly carry your brand,
- a seller or vendor account.

Because Amazon adjusts the exact requirements, check the current rules in the Brand Registry area before enrolling.

## The most important benefits

Brand Registry provides eligible brand owners with content, analytics and protection tools, subject to role and programme requirements. It can accept eligible pending as well as registered trademarks. It does not create an exclusive right to every catalogue field or justify removing legitimate resellers. Report substantiated infringement, not simply an unwanted competing offer. [Amazon Germany's brand-owner overview](https://sell.amazon.de/markeninhaber).

## The enrolment process

The process is manageable: you sign up in the Brand Registry portal, enter your trademark details, prove ownership (through the registration number and a verification step) and link your seller account. Amazon checks the details and unlocks the functions if it succeeds. The step that takes the most time is usually the trademark registration itself, so start that early, because depending on the country it can take months.

## Actually using the protection

Having the registry is not enough, you have to use the protection functions too. Watch your listings regularly for unauthorized co-sellers, image theft or counterfeit versions, and report breaches through the tools provided. For stronger protection, combine the registry with programmes such as Transparency (product authentication) when counterfeits are a serious issue in your category.

## Why it is worth it

Without Brand Registry you are largely defenceless on Amazon against free riders, and you lose access to the strongest selling and data tools. With it you build a real, defensible brand, one that not only sells better but is worth more and is more resilient, for instance when the business is later sold.

## In short

For any serious brand on Amazon, Brand Registry is not a nice-to-have but basic equipment. It unlocks A+ content, brand advertising, analytics and, decisively, protection tools against counterfeiters and listing thieves. The most important requirement is a registered trademark, and registering one takes time. Whoever enrols early and actively uses the protection functions lays the foundation for a brand that can be defended and built on.
