# Setting up an Amazon storefront: building your brand store from scratch

An Amazon storefront, your own brand shop inside Amazon, gives the brand a fixed place where buyers can experience your whole range, your story and your world. For brand-registered sellers it is free, and it is an important building block for brand building and for the funnel. This article walks you through setting up your first store.

## What a storefront is

The storefront is a multi-page brand presence on Amazon, comparable to a small website inside the platform but buildable without any coding. You can arrange products by theme, show brand content and guide visitors deliberately. Unlike a single listing, the store presents your whole brand rather than one product.

## The prerequisite: Brand Registry

To create a store you need Amazon Brand Registry, which means a registered trademark. Without it the tool is not available. If you are not registered yet, that is the first step, and alongside the store it also unlocks A+ content, Sponsored Brands and protection tools.

## Building it step by step

Amazon provides a store builder that works with ready-made blocks: tiles, images, product grids, video. Roughly it runs like this:

1. **Plan the structure:** before building, work out which pages you need, a home page plus sub-pages by category, use case or audience.
2. **Design the home page:** it is your shop window and your signpost at once. The strongest brand message belongs here, plus clear routes into the sub-pages.
3. **Create category pages:** arrange products the way customers think and search.
4. **Add content:** images, text, video and product links that carry the brand and the benefit.
5. **Check and publish:** test the preview, on mobile as well, and submit for approval.

## Think in terms of the funnel

The most common beginner mistake is to build the store as a mere product display. The funnel view works better: the home page routes visitors on according to their need, every page has a clear job and a next step. Think from the visitor's side. Where do you want to lead them, and what helps them decide? A well-guided store sells. A shop window does not.

## Think mobile first

A large share of visitors arrives by phone. Overloaded pages, small type or too many elements quickly become confusing on mobile. Keep the design clear, check every page in the mobile preview and make sure the most important messages land immediately on a small screen too.

## Drive traffic into the store

A finished store is of little use without visitors. Connect it to your advertising: Sponsored Brands ads can lead straight into the store or into matching sub-pages. External traffic (social media, newsletters) can be pointed at the store too and measured through Amazon Attribution. That makes the store the central landing surface of your brand.

## Measure and improve

Amazon provides store insights with visitor numbers, dwell time, sales per page and traffic sources. Use that data to see which pages work and where visitors drop off. A store is not a one-off project, it is a surface you improve continuously.

## Conclusion

An Amazon storefront gives your brand a home on the platform, free for brand-registered sellers and buildable without coding. The key to a successful store is to think of it as a funnel rather than a shop window, design it for mobile, feed it deliberately with traffic and improve it continuously through the insights. Cover those points and you create more than a good-looking page: a genuine selling space for your brand.
