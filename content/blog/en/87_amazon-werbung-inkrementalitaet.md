# Amazon advertising incrementality: separate attributed sales from additional demand

Amazon advertising incrementality asks how much business would not have happened without the advertising. Attribution asks which observed purchases qualify for credit under a reporting rule. Both can be useful, but they answer different questions.

A high attributed ROAS may include customers who already intended to buy. New-to-Brand identifies customers under a defined purchase-history rule; it does not establish that advertising caused their first purchase. Treat these metrics as descriptive evidence until the research design supports a causal conclusion.

## Define the intervention and comparison

Specify the audience, treatment, products, period and primary outcome before launch. Where a suitable controlled test is available, assignment should determine the comparison. Comparing people who happened to see an ad with those who did not can introduce selection bias.

Check statistical planning, likely effect size and contamination between groups. A small campaign cannot become conclusive merely by being called an experiment. Account access and feature availability determine what can actually be implemented.

## Translate the result into contribution

<aside class="article-example"><p class="example-label">Simplified controlled-test illustration</p><h3>Incremental revenue can still fail the profit test</h3><p>Two hypothetical groups each contain 10,000 people. One produces 600 orders and the other 500. Under valid comparability assumptions, the difference is 100 orders. At €40 net revenue per order, that is €4,000 incremental revenue. Against €1,000 media, incremental ROAS is 4. At 30% contribution before media, €1,200 remains; after media and €400 additional operating cost, the result is minus €200.</p></aside>

The arithmetic illustrates the economic bridge. It does not demonstrate statistical confidence or remove the need to verify randomisation, uncertainty and other costs. Keep the effect estimate and its uncertainty visible in the decision.

## Know what each measurement approach supports

| Evidence | Useful for | Does not establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Attributed ROAS | Reporting credited revenue against spend | Additional demand |
| New-to-Brand | Describing purchase history under the metric definition | Causality |
| Amazon Marketing Cloud analysis | Exploring observed paths and overlap | A valid counterfactual |
| Brand Lift | Assessing surveyed brand perceptions | Incremental product sales |

AMC can help investigate behaviour, but an observational analysis remains observational unless the design supports stronger inference. Brand Lift addresses perception rather than automatically measuring additional orders.

## Make uncertainty part of the decision

When a controlled design is unavailable, document the limitations of matched periods, markets or products. Seasonality, price, distribution and availability can all change outcomes. A simple before-and-after chart should not be presented as proof.

Agree what size of commercially credible effect would justify continuing.
