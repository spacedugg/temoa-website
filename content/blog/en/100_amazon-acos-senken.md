# Lower Amazon ACoS without losing profitable growth

ACoS can fall if you pause generic campaigns and serve only branded demand, even while new customer acquisition weakens. Examine each query and ASIN against contribution before lowering bids across the account.

## Separate the drivers

As a simplified diagnostic, ACoS equals CPC divided by click-to-order conversion times attributed revenue per order. Real reporting also needs attribution windows, order mix and delayed sales.

<aside class="article-example"><p class="example-label">Illustrative campaign</p><h3>Conversion changes the result</h3><p>At €1.20 CPC, ten percent conversion and €40 attributed order revenue, ad cost per order is €12 and ACoS is 30%. If conversion rises to 12% at the same CPC, modeled ACoS falls to 25%. At eight percent conversion it rises to 37.5%. Investigate price, Featured Offer, availability and content before changing a productive bid.</p></aside>

## An actionable weekly review

Separate branded, generic, competitor and product targeting. Audit search terms at adequate click and order volume. Exclude clearly irrelevant exact terms; a negative phrase may also remove useful variants. Move validated queries into controllable targets and preserve a capped discovery budget. Change one important variable at a time and compare total contribution, not campaign ACoS alone.
