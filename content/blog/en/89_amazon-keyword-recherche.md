# Amazon keyword research: prioritise purchase intent and product fit

Amazon keyword research becomes useful when it tells the team what to change. A long list of phrases is not a strategy. Established brands need to identify which searches fit the product, where evidence indicates a gap and whether the opportunity can be served profitably.

Start with the product rather than a search-volume export. Document actual dimensions, use cases, materials and exclusions. A popular query that promises an unsupported function can attract clicks while creating disappointment and returns.

## Keep evidence sources separate

Search Query Performance provides query-level context for eligible brand accounts. Advertising search-term reports describe traffic within paid campaigns. Customer questions and reviews reveal language and unresolved concerns. These sources have different populations and should not be merged as if they measured the same thing.

| Intent | Example decision |
|---|---|
| Category need | Is the product relevant enough to compete? |
| Specific use case | Can images clearly demonstrate suitability? |
| Compatibility | Is the claim supported by product facts? |
| Brand or model search | Does the correct variant appear clearly? |

## Prioritise fit before apparent demand

<aside class="article-example"><p class="example-label">Illustrative product decision</p><h3>A narrow organiser should not promise every use case</h3><p>A brand sells a narrow organiser for a specific cupboard depth. A broad storage query may attract many impressions, while a smaller query naming the relevant depth has stronger product fit. The content task is to show dimensions and the fitting situation clearly. Bidding more aggressively on every broad storage phrase does not solve that information gap.</p></aside>

Rank opportunities by product fit, evidence quality, unresolved customer questions and commercial importance. Avoid presenting an arbitrary weighted score as scientific precision. The purpose of prioritisation is to make the next action defensible.

## Assign each opportunity to the correct surface

Some phrases belong in natural product copy. Others point to a missing comparison image, an incomplete attribute or an advertising test. Respect current field requirements; our [title and Item Highlights guide](/blog/produkttitel-optimieren) covers the relevant title changes.

Do not force every phrase into the title. A compatibility question may be answered more clearly through accurate specifications and a visual explanation. A product mismatch should be excluded from the targeting plan rather than disguised through wording.

## Validate with a bounded test

Choose a small group of commercially meaningful queries. State the hypothesis, change, expected signal and maximum exposure. Allow for attribution delay and the expected conversion rate before judging paid traffic. A fixed ten-click stopping rule ignores large differences between products.

Use [Search Query Performance](/blog/search-query-performance), campaign reports and [content experiments](/blog/amazon-content-experimente) for their respective purposes. Changing all surfaces simultaneously makes it harder to identify what helped.
