# Positioning on Amazon: anchoring your product in the buyer's mind

In a marketplace with millions of products, the most important question is not "how do I sell more?" but "why should anybody choose me?". The answer to that is your positioning, the place your product occupies in the buyer's mind. Without clear positioning you are just another option in a long list that gets decided on price. This article explains how to find a position and make it visible in the listing.

## What positioning really is

Positioning is not your logo or your slogan. It is the one thing your product stands for, the reason a particular audience prefers it over another. Good positioning answers three questions clearly: who is the product for? Which problem does it solve better than others? And why is that credible? Whoever cannot answer those leaves the decision to chance, or to the lowest price.

## The mistake of trying to please everyone

The most common positioning mistake is being as broad as possible so as not to exclude anybody. The result is a product that fits everybody a little and nobody exactly. Strength comes from focus: a product that is the best choice for a clearly defined audience beats a generic product that offers everybody nothing but the middle. The courage to specialize is a competitive advantage.

## Ways to position yourself

There are several axes on which you can stand apart:

- **Audience:** made specifically for beginners, professionals, an age group, a way of living.
- **Use case:** built for one concrete situation (travel, the office, outdoors).
- **Quality and premium:** better materials, longer life, a better experience.
- **Solving a problem:** deliberately fixing a weak point buyers complain about with competitors.
- **Values:** sustainability, origin, how it is made, when credibly supported.

You often find the best axis in your competitors' reviews: where buyers are disappointed, your chance is waiting.

## Making the positioning visible in the listing

A position that exists only in your head has no effect. It has to shine through in every element of the listing: the title names the core benefit, the main image and the gallery show who and what the product is made for, the bullet points lay out exactly the advantages that support your position, and the A+ content tells the story behind it. Consistency is decisive, because every part of the listing should reinforce the same message.

## Positioning and price

A clear position gives you room on price. Whoever is seen as "the best choice for X" does not have to be the cheapest, and in fact too low a price can undermine a premium position. The other way round: whoever has no position can only compete on price and lands in a fight over margin. Positioning is therefore also a tool for protecting your margin.

## In short

Positioning is the strategic foundation everything else rests on: listing, advertising, price. It answers who your product is the best choice for and why. Whoever has the courage to focus, derives their position from the weaknesses of competitors and carries it consistently through the whole listing escapes interchangeability and pure price competition. In a crowded market, clarity about your own "why me" is the most valuable head start there is.
