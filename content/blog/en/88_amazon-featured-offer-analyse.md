# Amazon Featured Offer: investigate lost share without sacrificing margin

Amazon Featured Offer share is an operational signal, not a complete diagnosis. Losing the prominent offer position can affect sales while the product page remains visible. First establish what changed, for which ASIN, marketplace and delivery location.

For the EU and UK, Amazon announced removal of the previous eligibility requirement from 20 July 2026. This does not guarantee that your offer will be featured. Older US explanations of eligibility should not be applied unchanged to a German account.

## Distinguish three different situations

| Observation | First investigation |
|---|---|
| Your offer cannot be purchased | Availability, listing status and account messages |
| Another seller is featured | Comparable price, delivery promise and offer conditions |
| No offer is prominently featured | Current detail-page presentation and applicable account information |

Record time, location, device context and the exact offer. A single screenshot can miss variation across buyers or times of day. Compare the observation with account reports and the period over which sales changed.

## Inspect delivery before changing price

A longer delivery promise can change the competitive position even when the displayed item price is unchanged. Check available stock, fulfilment settings and delivery conditions. Separate the price of the item from the total amount paid by the customer.

Confirm whether the issue concerns one variation, a group of products or the entire account. A localised stock problem needs a different intervention from a wider operational issue. Keep changes and observations in one timeline.

<aside class="article-example"><p class="example-label">Simplified margin illustration</p><h3>A €3 price cut can require 50% more units</h3><p>At €30 net revenue and €21 variable costs, contribution is €9 per unit. A €3 reduction leaves €6 if costs are held constant. Maintaining total contribution then requires 1.5 times as many units. In practice, percentage-based fees and other costs may also change, so recalculate the actual economics.</p></aside>

The example does not predict a sales response. It shows why a price reduction needs an explicit economic test. Define a floor and an observation period before allowing repeated automatic cuts.

## Coordinate advertising and catalogue work

Check whether campaign delivery and conversion changed alongside offer share. Avoid expanding spend on a product whose availability or offer position is unresolved. At the same time, do not assume every lost advertising sale was caused by the Featured Offer.

Distinguish legitimate resale from inaccurate product content and actual rights infringements. Brand tools do not automatically entitle a brand to remove every other seller of genuine goods.

## Build a repeatable incident record

For each material change, record the ASIN, market, affected dates, observed offer, fulfilment conditions, contribution and action owner. Recheck after the intervention before declaring the problem resolved.
