# Comparing Amazon marketing agencies: define the work first

The right partner depends on what your brand already does well. List the operational gaps across advertising, content, inventory, account health and analysis. For each one require a named owner, an actual deliverable and a decision cadence.

## Compare operating models

A PPC specialist can focus on campaign control but may not own a weak detail page or stockout. A full-service partner can coordinate these dependencies, provided responsibilities and approvals are clear. Software helps automate parts of the workflow; a team must still interpret tradeoffs.

<aside class="article-example"><p class="example-label">Illustrative selection</p><h3>The cheaper retainer may leave expensive work undone</h3><p>A brand spending €80,000 monthly on ads compares €4,000 PPC management with a €7,000 offer that also coordinates content and FBA. The €3,000 difference is worthwhile only if the additional work is delivered and its effect or saved internal effort can be assessed. Request the same first-90-day plan, staffing and data rights from both bidders.</p></aside>

## Six questions for a pitch

Ask for an account diagnosis with evidence; the actual team and cover; 30-, 60- and 90-day outputs; access to raw data and SKU contribution; ownership of campaigns and creative after termination; and a complete scope and exit terms. Provider names or a published ranking cannot determine fit.
